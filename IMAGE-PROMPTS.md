# IMAGE-PROMPTS.md
## Photographic Art Direction & AI Image Prompts for Hafiz Milk Shop (Est. 1928, Lahore)

This document specifies the exact photographic briefs for generating realistic, heritage-grounded imagery for **Hafiz Milk Shop**. 

> **CRITICAL ART DIRECTION RULES (Strictly Enforced):**
> 1. **Style**: Documentary food editorial photography, natural soft northern window light, 35mm-50mm prime lens aesthetic, shallow depth of field (f/2.2–f/2.8), warm muted organic grade.
> 2. **Texture & Atmosphere**: Visible authentic grain in granular clarified ghee, matte chilled block butter, aged Punjabi teak counter, dented vintage tin, hand-hammered brass spoons, muslin strainers, clay earthenware.
> 3. **Context**: Authentic old Lahore dairy aesthetic (Garhi Shahu / Walled City tradition). No Western brunch, no sourdough boards, no sterile factory laboratories.
> 4. **Forbidden**: NO typography, NO legible Urdu/English text or labels (generators distort text), NO human faces/eyes, NO CGI gloss, NO neon lighting, NO plastic wrap, NO cartoonish colors, NO HDR halos.
> 5. **Client Boundaries**: Do NOT generate photos of the shopfront, staff, or family elders. Those sections remain reserved for real historical archive photos supplied by the Hafiz family.
